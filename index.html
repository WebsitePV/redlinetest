About Us

Redline Capital

Redline Capital specialises in the acquisition and sale of exclusive automobiles. Our curated collection features only the finest machines, selected on authenticity, rarity and condition. Every car in our collection has a story worth telling. We make sure it gets told.

Powered by a passion for extraordinary automobiles and driven to build a legacy, we aim to redefine the art of collecting these extraordinary pieces. Because legacy is not inherited, it is built over time.

For us, that means showing up. It means taking these machines to the places they were built to go, and documenting every mile of the journey. It means building a story worth telling, one drive at a time, with a collection worth remembering.

The Collection

Redline Capital represents a carefully curated collection of seven extraordinary Ferrari models, each selected for its provenance, character and timeless design. Our collection spans four decades of Ferrari's finest engineering, from iconic classics to contemporary masterpieces. From the raw, analogue intensity of the F40 to the refined elegance of the Roma Spider and the naturally aspirated V12 of the 12 Cilindri, each machine represents a distinct era and a distinct expression of what Ferrari does best.

Centrepiece
Ferrari
F40
1989
The F40 is widely regarded as one of the greatest Ferraris ever built. Conceived by Enzo Ferrari himself, it was the last model he personally approved before his passing. Raw, uncompromising and built purely for the road and track, the F40 set a new benchmark for what a road car could be. Its twin-turbocharged V8, stripped interior and near-total lack of driver aids make it an analogue experience unlike anything built before or since.
Engine2.9L Twin-Turbo V8
Output478 bhp
ColourRosso Corsa
Production1,311 worldwide
Ferrari
Testarossa Nero
1989
The Testarossa defined an era. Its wide, straked flanks and flat-12 engine became icons of 1980s automotive design. This particular example, finished in rare Nero black, stands apart from the predominantly red production run. Few Testarossas exist in this specification, making it a genuinely distinctive piece of Ferrari history.
Engine4.9L Flat-12
Output390 bhp
ColourNero (rare spec)
Ferrari
550 Maranello
1999
The 550 Maranello marked Ferrari's return to the front-engined V12 gran turismo after the mid-engined Testarossa generation. Widely praised by drivers and critics alike, it combined genuine performance with everyday usability. Its naturally aspirated 5.5-litre V12 remains one of the finest engines the company has ever produced.
Engine5.5L V12
Output485 bhp
ColourTour de France Blue
Ferrari
575 Superamerica
2005
The 575 Superamerica is one of the most technically unusual Ferraris ever built. Its defining feature is an electrochromic rotating glass roof panel. Produced in only 559 examples, it represents one of the more adventurous chapters in Ferrari's gran turismo story.
Engine5.7L V12
Output540 bhp
Production559 worldwide
Ferrari
GTC4 Lusso
2020
The GTC4 Lusso is Ferrari's most practical car. Its shooting brake body, four-wheel drive system and rear-seat configuration make it the only Ferrari genuinely suited to year-round use. Beneath the practicality sits a 6.3-litre naturally aspirated V12 that produces 680 horsepower and one of the most memorable engine sounds in the modern Ferrari range.
Engine6.3L V12
Output690 bhp

Ferrari
Roma Spider
2024
The Roma Spider takes its name and spirit from the golden age of Italian la dolce vita. Where many modern Ferraris lean into aggression, the Roma takes a different path of understated elegance and effortless style. Its turbocharged V8 and retractable hard top make it one of the most complete open-top Ferraris in recent memory.
Engine3.9L Twin-Turbo V8
Output620 bhp
ColourCeleste Blue
Ferrari
12 Cilindri Coupé
2025
The 12 Cilindri represents Ferrari's commitment to the naturally aspirated V12 at a time when the industry is moving away from large displacement engines. Its 6.5-litre unit produces 830 horsepower and revs to 9,500 rpm. A car that will be looked back on as the last of its kind.

The Journey

The Road to the Mille Miglia

The Mille Miglia is one of the most iconic and celebrated events in automotive history. The original race ran from 1927 to 1957, covering roughly 1,600 kilometres across Italy from Brescia to Rome and back. It was not a race on a closed circuit. It was a race through towns, over mountain passes and along coastal roads, open to the public, unpredictable and unlike anything that existed before or since.

Today the Mille Miglia lives on as a regularity rally for historic cars. The route remains largely the same. The roads are the same. And the machines that take part are the same machines that defined an era of automotive history.

For Redline Capital, taking the F40 to the Mille Miglia is exactly what this collection is about. A 36-year-old Ferrari, built without compromise, driven through Italy the way it was meant to be driven. Every kilometre of it documented.

This is the story of how we got there.

Chapter 01

The Car

The Ferrari F40 needs little introduction. Built between 1987 and 1992 to celebrate Ferrari's 40th anniversary, it was the last model personally approved by Enzo Ferrari before his passing. Twin-turbocharged V8, carbon and Kevlar bodywork, no carpet, no sound system, no unnecessary weight. Just the engine, the chassis and the driver.

Of the 1,311 examples ever produced, very few have lived the life they were built for. This one will.

Chapter 02

The Preparation

Every detail matters when you put a 36-year-old Ferrari on the road for 1,600 kilometres through Italy. That means more than a visual inspection.

The F40 went in for a full check-up at Kroymans Ferrari, one of the most respected Ferrari specialists in the Netherlands. Every system checked, every detail verified. The kind of preparation that gives you confidence when you point the car south towards Italy.

The car passed. It was ready.

Chapter 03

Next Stop: Italy

The preparation was done. The car was cleared. Next stop: Brescia.

Loading an F40 onto a transporter is a moment in itself. A machine built to be driven, strapped down and shipped south. It feels almost wrong. But getting it to the start line in perfect condition is part of the respect you owe a car like this.

The F40 left the Netherlands on its way to Brescia, where the Mille Miglia begins. From there, the roads take over.

Chapter 04

Brescia — Rome

The F40 is on its way to Brescia. The Mille Miglia starts Tuesday. From there, 1,600 kilometres through Italy await. We will be documenting every moment. From the start line in Brescia to the finish in Rome and back. Stay tuned.
